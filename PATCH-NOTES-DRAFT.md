**We Ride at Dawn — Patch Notes**

The nightly PvP league is now the main event — the depth run still pays out scrap, but it's a farming lane feeding your board, not where you're measured anymore. Bragging rights live in the arena now.

**New: Nightly PvP League**
The Boss Trial is retired — replaced with a nightly PvP league. Every night your board gets scored against the rest of the warren instead of an escalating solo boss.
- **Season** and **Nights** tabs for overall standing vs. each individual night.
- A **scout panel** to check out other players' boards.
- **Duel replays** with 1x/2x/4x speed controls.
- Even a loss earns a small scrap consolation payout.

**Relic rework**
- **Rusted Nail** retired.
- **Glass Shard**: now +4 damage on your first attack, and punches straight through armor — the new answer to armored tanks.
- **Filth Totem**: reworked from a small health buff into +1 armor for the whole team.
- Shops now offer **1 relic slot instead of 2**.

**Unit changes**
- **Rat-Piper** reworked: summons one scaled-up pup at the start of battle instead of drip-feeding pups every wave — and that pup inherits a copy of Piper's own relic.
- **Gnawer** retired to make room for the reworked Rat-Piper.
- **Gutter-Acolyte** remade into the roster's first poison counter (blunts incoming poison damage for your side).
- **MBP Rat** replaces Slink-Rat as this season's prestige pick.
- **Draughtsman Moe** now unlocks day 2 instead of day 1.
- **Dire-Rat** and **Ward-Weaver** no longer held back to day 2 — full shop open from day 1.

Numbers are a first pass and may get tuned as we watch the new league in action.
